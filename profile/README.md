## What we're building

### FORGE-ECC — Engineering Control Center

A workspace for bringing AI engineering workflows into one place, with a focus on visibility, review, and control.

- **Run visibility** — inspect workflow runs and their history.
- **Human oversight** — review approval points and controlled actions.
- **Traceability** — follow execution traces and verification history.
- **Quality workflows** — organize reviews, benchmarks, and checks.
- **Workspace control** — explore workspaces and configure agents and workflows.
- **Operations** — access run controls and provider diagnostics.
